Your header is now **larger, more spacious, and easier to read** with full-width utilization.

## Top Bar Updates (Contact & Accreditation)

### Before:
- Compact: py-3 (12px padding)

- Small icons: h-3.5 w-3.5

## Readability Improvements

### 1. **Better Contrast**
- Larger text is easier to see
- More white space reduces eye strain
- Bold fonts improve legibility

### 2. **Clear Hierarchy**
```

↓ NABL Accredited Badge (Orange, Bold)
↓ Company Name "GOODNESS LABTECH" (Large, Bebas Neue)
↓ Contact Info (Phone & Email)
↓ Navigation Links
↓ ISO Certification
↓ Tagline
LEAST PROMINENT (Smallest)
```

### 3. **Better Spacing**
- More breathing room between elements
- Easier to click/tap on mobile
- Reduced clutter

### 4. **Professional Appearance**
- Larger = more confident
- Spacious = modern and clean
- Bold = authoritative

## Design Philosophy

### From Compact to Comfortable

**Old Approach:** Fit everything in minimal space
**New Approach:** Use space generously for clarity

**Benefits:**
1. **Easier to read** - Larger text, better contrast
2. **Better UX** - Bigger click targets
3. **More professional** - Modern, spacious design
4. **Brand prominence** - Key info stands out
5. **Accessibility** - Better for all users
