📸 Photography Tips & Guidelines

  • Resolution: Minimum 1920px width for hero images, 1200px for others
  • Format: JPG or WebP (WebP recommended for better performance)
  • Lighting: Well-lit, professional-looking images work best
  • Composition: Avoid cluttered backgrounds, focus on equipment and cleanliness
  • People: If including people, ensure proper safety gear and professional appearance
  • Branding: Ensure images align with professional laboratory aesthetic

🖼️ Where to Get Images

Option 1: Professional Photography (Recommended)

  • Hire a photographer for custom shots of your actual facility
  • Shows authenticity and builds trust with clients

Option 2: Stock Photography (Quick Start)

  • Unsplash - Free high-quality images
  • Pexels - Free stock photos
  • Search terms: "laboratory", "scientific testing", "quality control", "material testing"

Option 3: Your Own Photos

  • Use a good smartphone camera or DSLR
  • Natural lighting or proper lab lighting
  • Multiple angles of equipment and facility
